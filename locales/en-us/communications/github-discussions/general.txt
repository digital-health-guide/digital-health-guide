Say hello and tell us how you use Digital Health Guide

Welcome to the Digital Health Guide Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the guide.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Digital Health Guide because I wanted a practical handbook of best practices for delivering digital services in health and social care organizations to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** What is the hardest part of delivering digital services in your health or care setting?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.

## Where other topics belong

- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Errors or broken links: Issues (https://github.com/digital-health-guide/digital-health-guide/issues)
