Welcome to Digital Health Guide Discussions

Digital Health Guide now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Digital Health Guide is a practical handbook of best practices for delivering digital services in health and social care organizations. It is written for chief executives, directors of digital and transformation, product and delivery leads, clinical and care professionals, and programme managers.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers from other readers.
- You can suggest topics, fixes, and improvements before anyone writes them.
- You can share how you use the guide.

## Where to go for what

- Questions about using the guide: Q&A
- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Quick community votes: Polls
- Things you have built or tried with the guide: Show and tell
- Everything else: General
- Errors, broken links, or typos: Issues (https://github.com/digital-health-guide/digital-health-guide/issues)
